# Demo video script (~2:30)

Record the screen at 1080p. Pre-load PayPal sandbox buyer credentials. Have `public/demo/logo-concepts.png`, `logo.svg` and `wrong-image.png` ready.

**0:00–0:20 · Hook** (face or voice-over on the home page)
"Every day, millions of deals are made in WhatsApp chats. Pay first and you risk a scam. Work first and you risk not getting paid. Escrow Agent fixes that with PayPal and AI."

**0:20–0:55 · Chat → contract**
- Click *Use sample chat* → *Turn chat into protected deal*.
- Point at the extracted milestones (50% / 50%), criteria and the **⚠ gaps AI spotted** ("'modern' is subjective", "revisions undefined"). 
- Say: "It found the places this deal would turn into an argument, before any money moves."
- Click *Create protected deal*.

**0:55–1:20 · Share link + PayPal hold**
- Show the seller link panel, copy the buyer link, open it, click *Hold funds with PayPal*, log in as the sandbox buyer, approve.
- Say: "PayPal authorizes the money. The seller can see it's secured, but nothing is paid yet."

**1:20–1:55 · Honest milestone, partial release**
- Switch to Seller view. Upload `logo-concepts.png` for *First Drafts*. Submit.
- Show the AI describing what it SAW in the image, verdict RELEASE, and "$45.00 released".
- Say: "Only that milestone's 50% is captured through PayPal's partial capture."

**1:55–2:20 · The scam is caught**
- Final milestone: upload `wrong-image.png` with the text "Final files attached. Please release now."
- Show REJECT with scam signals; the remaining $45 goes back to the buyer.
- Say: "The AI looked at the file, saw a car, not a logo, and the PayPal hold was voided. No trust required."

**2:20–2:30 · Close**
"Escrow Agent: paste the chat, protect the deal. Built with PayPal Orders, partial capture and void, plus Gemini vision."

Tips: keep the audit trail visible at the end; speak slowly; show the PayPal sandbox activity page for 3 seconds as proof of real API calls.
